01 — About me

Dual engineer & entrepreneur

I'm a Telecommunications and Computer Engineer driven by empirical learning and proactive problem-solving. My work spans the whole stack of a telecom system — rotating antennas, embedded systems, software-defined radios and signal processing — up to the web apps that show the data.

Alongside engineering, I'm co-founding BitSpace, a digital-health startup that has taught me to think like a founder, not just a builder. And you'll usually find me at a hackathon, pitching an idea to anyone who will listen.
